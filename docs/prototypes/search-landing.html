How should search results and direct links land on a comment?

Search for “line ending”, open a result, and watch where you land. Three variants differ in the result row and in the landing. Pick a scenario to see a comment beyond the first page, a deleted comment, a deleted task or an archived task. Nothing here calls the real app.

Questions to settle

  3. For an older comment: show it with two neighbours on each side and gap bars (A, C), or show it alone in the reader with “Show it in history” (B)?
  4. Highlight: a 3-second tint with a lasting “From search” marker (A), selection only (B), or a banner until dismissed (C)?
  5. Should reopening search keep the last query and selected row? Today the palette clears on open; C keeps them.
