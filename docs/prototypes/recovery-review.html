Three variants of where recovery lives, how its review panel looks, and how items that are moving, pending deletion or blocked show up. The workspace below has four unfinished operations. Open them from the top bar, a board, Current work or the task itself. What each variant proposes

Questions to react to

  1. Where should recovery live: a top-bar drawer with a dialog (A), its own page with a workspace banner (B), or on the item itself with a status entry in the rail (C)? Or a mix, such as A's top-bar entry with B's page?
  2. Comment conflict: stacked versions (A), side by side (B), or one version at a time with a switch (C)?
  3. Structural problem: a path table with numbered repair steps (A, B), or a step timeline showing where it stopped (C)?
  4. Hidden items on the board: a dashed placeholder card in its column (A), one notice above the board (B), or a "Pending" strip under the columns (C)?
  5. Abandon confirmation: inline under the panel, with focus starting on Keep reviewing (A, B), or a separate small dialog (C)?
  6. After abandoning: should the item stay in the recovery list as Abandoned until dismissed, or leave it at once and show the final state only where you are?
